## Tokenrice — AI Model Selection & Pricing Workspace

The professional workspace for evaluating, comparing, and selecting AI models. Built for engineering, product, and procurement teams navigating the expanding landscape of AI providers.

- Model Catalog — Filterable, searchable database with side-by-side comparison
- 5-Dimension Scoring — Price, performance, latency, stability, provider reliability
- Strategy Builder — Draft, simulate, and publish model selection policies with cost projections
- Pricing Trends — Historical pricing data and spend simulation


## TapInFlow — AI-Powered Audience Interaction Platform

Drop in a topic, get AI-generated questions, collect live responses, and walk away with actionable insights. Replaces static polling tools with an intelligent feedback loop.

- AI Question Generation — Auto-creates contextual questions from topics, slides, or documents
- Real-Time Results — WebSocket-powered live charts, word clouds, and gauges
- Fullscreen Presentation Mode — Built for the big screen, keyboard-navigable
- AI Insights & Reports — Clustering, sentiment analysis, and follow-up suggestions
